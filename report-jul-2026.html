ReadThe gap keeps closing, month over month. Real revenue moved from 53.7% of platform-reported to 71.7% this month, and the overstatement multiple narrowed from 1.86× to 1.40×, with no new fix beyond the Jul 8 UTM/auto-tagging change continuing to mature.
Why this gap exists, and why it's fixableUnlike a permanent tracking limit, this is a configuration issue that was already fixed. A missing UTM auto-tagging setup meant WooCommerce couldn't cleanly separate Google Ads paid clicks from other Google-referred traffic. Fixed Jul 8 by adding tracking tags to every campaign. The remaining gap mostly reflects orders still landing in a generic "Google" bucket without a clear product on the order (a separate blank-Product(s) data issue, not a tracking failure), so expect this multiple to keep shrinking as more post-fix orders accumulate.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 447 completed orders Jul 1–31, 362 May 31–Jun 30. Both periods span exactly 31 calendar days. Refunded orders (7-8 per period) excluded entirely: WooCommerce's export lists each as two rows (an original-sale row and a separate negative reversal row), so including them would have double-counted rather than netting to zero.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-verified. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search) is never summed into this total, under any framing, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Sand+Stones and Compost revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded from Google's real-revenue total: no Google Ads campaign targets either category, so this is confirmed misattribution.
  • This gap is fixable, not permanent: a missing UTM/auto-tagging configuration, fixed Jul 8, not a permanent tracking limit. Expect the overstatement multiple to keep narrowing as more post-fix orders accumulate.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every Google real-vs-platform ratio. It's a quote-based install service, not a WooCommerce checkout product, so it has zero orders to verify against. Its €12,000-per-conversion value is a manual ADD rule, Ciaran-confirmed but never checked against a real closed deal.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting API (GAQL) for the exact Jul 1–31 / May 31–Jun 30 windows.
