Headline Notes
  • ▸Real revenue is up 91% and real profit is up 53% month over month. €152,125 in verified revenue for Jul 1–25 vs €79,665 for the same 25 days in June, with real profit (after your category margins) growing from roughly €17,450 to €26,660.
  • ▸Google Ads' reporting accuracy improved a lot this month. The gap between what Google's dashboard reports and what real orders confirm nearly halved, from Google overstating by 2.32x in June to just 1.32x in July. That's the UTM tracking fix from July 8 doing its job.
  • ▸Meta's dashboard still significantly overstates real results. This is a known, structural limitation (visitors who decline marketing cookies can't be tracked by any method currently in place), not a bug being chased. It means Meta's own reported ROAS shouldn't be used to judge performance until CAPI (in progress with Graphedia) is live.
  • ▸Combined, the business swung from roughly breakeven to solidly profitable on a real, verified basis, driven mainly by Google Ads.

⚠️
Why this gap exists, and what fixes it
  • This is privacy-driven: visitors who decline marketing cookies can't be tracked by Meta's pixel at all, by design, under GDPR. It's the same limitation across the industry, not something specific to your account.
  • June's ~170x reading is a very small sample (just 1 confirmed real order that month) so treat the exact multiple loosely, but the direction is the same both months: Meta's own dashboard cannot be trusted for budget or creative decisions right now.
  • The fix is Conversions API (CAPI), a server-side tracking method that doesn't rely on the visitor's browser. This is in progress with Graphedia (your web developer); we'll flag as soon as it's live and this gap starts closing.
