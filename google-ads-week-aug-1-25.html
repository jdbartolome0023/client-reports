Firewood budget change

Daily budget raised from €20 to €30 on August 19, funded by cutting Kenergy Solar from €15 to €5
Solar was losing the large majority of its impressions to rank, not budget: it was averaging only around €2.37 of its old €15 daily cap, so the extra room wasn't being used. Firewood, by contrast, was budget-capped and converting well above breakeven. Moving €10 a day from Solar to Firewood put spend where it was actually constrained. The category table above confirms it: Firewood orders doubled and real profit grew to €3,603.58 this period. Holding at €30 a day for a few more days to confirm the trend continues before considering a further increase.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 391 valid orders Aug 1–25, 384 Jul 1–25 (refunded/cancelled/failed orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-verified. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search) is never summed into this total, under any framing, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost, Sand+Stones, and Coal/solid fuel revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded from Google's real-revenue total: no Google Ads campaign targets any of these categories, so this is confirmed misattribution.
  • One honest caveat: about a third of orders (125 of 391 this period) don't carry product details in the export, so category-level margin figures carry some real uncertainty. The channel split (Google vs Meta vs Direct) is solid regardless, that part doesn't depend on product data.
  • Category fingerprinting: blank-Product(s) orders matched to a named category only where corroborated by a signal (the `kenny10` coupon code for Heating Oil, or a non-overlapping value range against 3+ known orders in the same category and period).
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every Google real-vs-platform ratio. It's a quote-based install service, not a WooCommerce checkout product, so it has zero orders to verify against. Its €12,000-per-conversion ADD rule remains paused pending a real closed-deal confirmation.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting API (GAQL) for the exact Aug 1–25 / Jul 1–25 windows.
