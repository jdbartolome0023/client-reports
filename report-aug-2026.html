ReadReal verified revenue grew 28.9% month over month (€61,306.15 to €78,995.49), the strongest verified month since I took the account over. Google's own reported value grew faster still (+63.6%), which widened the overstatement multiple from 1.58x to 2.01x.
Why the multiple widenedPart of this is a good sign, not a problem. Since the mid-August fix on Brand Search (removing non-brand traffic), Google's conversion value on that campaign has climbed because genuine website purchases are now being recorded there, not just phone calls at a flat value. So some of the platform-side growth reflects real sales. I am treating the wider multiple as a figure to keep watching, not a fix to chase, since the verified number underneath it is also up.
Full contextKenny Fuels took €201,767 in website sales across all channels in August (531 orders), up from €174,046 in July (447 orders). Of August's total, €78,995 is independently WooCommerce-attributed to Google Ads (the verified figure above), against €158,817 that Google's own dashboard claims for the same period.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 531 valid orders Aug 1–31, 447 Jul 1–31 (refunded orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-verified. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search) is never summed into this total, under any framing, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost, Sand+Stones, and Coal/solid fuel revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded from Google's real-revenue total: no Google Ads campaign targets any of these categories, so this is confirmed misattribution.
  • One honest caveat: about a third of orders don't carry product details in the export, so category-level margin figures carry some real uncertainty. The channel split (Google vs Meta vs Direct) is solid regardless, that part doesn't depend on product data.
  • Category fingerprinting: blank-product orders matched to a named category only where corroborated by a signal (the first-order discount code for Heating Oil, an oil price-comparison referral, or a non-overlapping value range against three or more known orders in the same category and month).
  • July comparison: July includes a four-day heating-oil price event (Jul 8 to 13) that lifted that month's revenue. August is shown against the full July month, with no equivalent event of its own.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every Google real-versus-platform ratio. It is a quote-based install service, not a WooCommerce checkout product, so it has zero orders to verify against. Its €12,000 per conversion ADD rule remains paused pending a real closed-deal confirmation.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting query language for the exact Aug 1–31 and Jul 1–31 windows. ROAS means return on ad spend.
