The headline

ReadThe first week of heating season swung Google Ads from a real loss to a real profit, on lower spend. Spend came down 12% period over period, real Google-driven revenue nearly quadrupled (€5,494.92 to €22,394.22) as heating oil demand returned, and real net profit went from a loss of around €850 in the first week of August to a profit of €1,447 to €1,574 in the first week of September. Brand Search did this on 41% less spend than the same week last month, the clearest payoff yet from the August account cleanup.

Ad spend, answered directly

ReadGoogle spend came down again this period. €1,317.32 (ex-Solar) over the first six days of September, 12.1% below the same six days in August, even with the four new Shopping campaigns going live on September 1 (they added €286 of the total). The four core Search campaigns actually spent €185 less than a month ago. Combined with Meta, which was deliberately dialled down to near-zero from September 4, total spend across both platforms is running well under the approved budget.

What changed this period

September 1: four Google Shopping campaigns went live
Gas, Solid Fuel, Fencing, and Hardware, at €10–15/day each (€45/day combined). These cover product searches the text-ad campaigns don't reach. Week one is a ramp: impressions are climbing (Hardware and Fencing are each showing 5,000–10,000 times a day), spend is tracking to budget, and real orders are just starting (Solid Fuel and Hardware have their first). Judged on real orders per euro at the two-week mark.
September 1: Home Heating Oil budget restored to €50/day for the season
It had been cut to €25/day over summer and was losing roughly half its available impressions to budget. Back at €50/day for heating season.
September 3: every campaign now optimises toward real website sales
The account had been set up so that phone calls (valued at a flat €90) and cart events counted as conversions alongside real purchases. Campaigns were reviewed one by one and pointed at website purchases as the goal. This is the main reason Brand Search's efficiency jumped.
September 7: Firewood, Solar and Composite Decking moved to full-week scheduling
These three campaigns had been on a narrow ad schedule and are now set to serve every day, ahead of the firewood-season ramp. Impressions on all three should build back over the following few days.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 136 valid orders Sep 1–6, 72 Aug 1–6 (refunded/cancelled/failed orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 44 orders / €18,021.52 this period) is never summed into this total, under any framing, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand+Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded: no Google Ads campaign targets these categories. Solid fuel is included from Sep 1 (new Shopping campaign); it carries no margin figure as none has been supplied.
  • Category fingerprinting: about 40% of orders don't carry product details in the export, overwhelmingly heating oil (kerosene, single line item, priced per litre). Single-item blank orders in the kerosene value band are classified as heating oil; the export's own attribution and value data is unaffected.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%, compost/sand 25%). Solid fuel has no supplied margin and is excluded from all profit math.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio. Its €12,000-per-conversion ADD rule remains paused pending a real closed-deal confirmation.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting API (GAQL) for the exact Sep 1–6 / Aug 1–6 windows.
