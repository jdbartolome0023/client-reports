The one thing to get straight first: which "ROAS"
PLATFORMWhat the Google and Meta dashboards show (about 28x and 19x in August). This is not a real number. Google counts phone calls at a flat 90 to 220 euro each, and until mid-August it counted solar leads at 12,000 euro each. Meta over-reports purchases by roughly ten times because of the cookie-consent tracking gap. A high platform figure sitting next to "we lost money" is this mismatch, not a contradiction.
REALVerified revenue from your actual WooCommerce orders, divided by what was spent on ads. In August that was 13.8x on Google and 1.9x on Meta.
NETThe only figure that answers whether the account is making money: real verified revenue, at your real product margins, minus the ad spend. Everything in this report is built on this.

The decision point on Meta
By the end of September: fix the measurement, or restructure the spend
Meta's loss is partly real and partly that the site cannot currently record every Meta-driven sale. The fix that would settle which is a website-side change on the Conversions API, with Niall at Graphedia since early August. If it lands and confirms Meta is actually profitable, the plan is to hold and scale the verified winners. If it does not land, or it lands and Meta is still a verified loss, the plan is to cut Meta back to the parts that are independently confirmed to make money (Firewood, which has real evidence every month) and stop the rest until the measurement is trustworthy. Either way, no more months of an unexplained Meta loss.

Risks and dependencies

  • DependencyThe Conversions API fix (Niall / Graphedia). This is the single biggest unknown. It is the difference between knowing Meta's real result and guessing at it. It has been open since early August.
  • TechnicalFirewood search on Google stopped serving August 23. A bid-setup change was made on September 1 that should restore it. If it does not recover in a couple of days I will take it to Google support directly.
  • MarketHeating season demand. The plan assumes a normal seasonal increase from September into winter. A mild autumn would soften heating oil and solid fuel.
  • UnprovenGoogle Shopping. New channel, no track record on this account. The plan is to scale only what produces real orders, so the downside is capped at the small starting budget.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Real revenue: WooCommerce "Net Sales" (excluding VAT and shipping), matched to the channel by the site's own attribution tags, product category, and coupon codes.
  • Real net profit: real verified revenue multiplied by your real product margins (heating oil 10 percent, gas 20 percent, hardware and fencing 15 percent, firewood and compost 25 percent), minus the platform-reported ad spend.
  • Ranges, not single numbers: about a third of orders do not carry product detail in the export, so category margins carry some uncertainty. The channel split (Google versus Meta versus Direct) is solid regardless.
  • Kenergy Solar is excluded from all profit maths: it is a quote-based install service with no online checkout to verify against.
  • Full monthly detail: the August Google Ads and Meta Ads reports carry the campaign-level breakdown behind these totals.
