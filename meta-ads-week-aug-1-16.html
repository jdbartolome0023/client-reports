ReadThe gap widened again this period (12.5× to 17.4×), continuing the pattern from the Aug 1-9 report rather than reversing it. CAPI was only confirmed live Aug 6-10, so this window is the first real test of whether it moves the number - it hasn't yet, at least not on the account-wide overstatement multiple. The known, still-open coverage gap (Niall's server not sending Click ID/fbc, flagged Aug 10) is the most likely reason match quality hasn't improved the headline figure yet.
Why this gap existsThis is an overstatement problem, not a missing-events problem. Meta's pixel is actively receiving and recording events, it just can't distinguish genuine purchases from other signals for consent-declined visitors, so it reports too many. Partly permanent, not fully solvable even by CAPI: visitors who decline marketing cookies cannot legally be tracked for ad attribution under Irish ePrivacy/GDPR rules (Graphedia confirmed Jul 7). CAPI only improves match quality for visitors who already consented, it cannot recover anyone who declined. This isn't purely consent-decline either: only 5 of 176 total site orders this period carry any Meta or paid-social attribution tag at all. The verified figure is also a floor, not an exact count: WooCommerce's own attribution engine is itself consent-gated, so some genuine Meta-driven purchases may be mistagged as Direct or Organic - confirmed directly this session on the Gas Cylinders campaign specifically (zero of 37 real Gas orders this window carried any Meta tag, despite Meta's own pixel recording 22 real purchases on that campaign since Jul 1).

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 176 valid orders Aug 1–16, 277 Jul 1–16 (refunded orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged "Source: [DV]..." (campaign-name tags) or "Source: Paid+Social" counted as Meta-verified. Organic, Direct, and other referral traffic excluded, even where it happens to mention Meta or Facebook.
  • Permanent limitation: visitors who decline marketing cookies cannot legally be tracked by any method under GDPR/ePrivacy rules. No fix exists for this specific piece, including CAPI. CAPI (Conversions API, confirmed live Aug 6-10) only improves match quality for visitors who already consented - a separate, smaller improvement, not a resolution to the consent-decline gap.
  • The verified figure is a floor, not an exact count: confirmed directly this session on Gas Cylinders - WooCommerce's own attribution engine (Sourcebuster JS) is itself gated behind "Statistics" cookie consent, so some genuinely Meta-driven purchases land as Direct or Organic instead.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Meta's own reporting API for the exact Aug 1–16 / Jul 1–16 windows.
