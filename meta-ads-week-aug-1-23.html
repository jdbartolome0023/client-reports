ReadThe gap is essentially flat, sitting inside the same 8.9%-14.2% real-vs-platform band every check has shown since Jul 14. This is the same period CAPI's fbc/Click ID coverage fix was requested from Niall (Aug 10) - no confirmation yet that it's landed, and the headline overstatement multiple hasn't moved either direction, consistent with the fix still being outstanding.
Why this gap existsThis is an overstatement problem, not a missing-events problem. Meta's pixel is actively receiving and recording events, it just can't distinguish genuine purchases from other signals for consent-declined visitors, so it reports too many. Partly permanent, not fully solvable even by CAPI: visitors who decline marketing cookies cannot legally be tracked for ad attribution under Irish ePrivacy/GDPR rules (Graphedia confirmed Jul 7). CAPI only improves match quality for visitors who already consented, it cannot recover anyone who declined. The verified figure is also a floor, not an exact count: WooCommerce's own attribution engine is itself consent-gated, so some genuine Meta-driven purchases may be mistagged as Direct or Organic - this is exactly why every figure above is shown as a floor-to-ceiling range rather than one number.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 323 valid orders Aug 1–23, 353 Jul 1–23 (refunded orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged "Source: [DV]..." (campaign-name tags), "Source: Paid+Social", "Source: Ig", "Source: Fb", or a facebook.com referral counted as Meta-verified. Organic, Direct, and other referral traffic excluded, even where it happens to mention Meta or Facebook.
  • Permanent limitation: visitors who decline marketing cookies cannot legally be tracked by any method under GDPR/ePrivacy rules. No fix exists for this specific piece, including CAPI. CAPI (Conversions API, confirmed live Aug 10) only improves match quality for visitors who already consented - a separate, smaller improvement, not a resolution to the consent-decline gap.
  • The verified figure is a floor, not an exact count: WooCommerce's own attribution engine (Sourcebuster JS) is itself gated behind "Statistics" cookie consent, so some genuinely Meta-driven purchases land as Direct or Organic instead.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Meta's own reporting API for the exact Aug 1–23 / Jul 1–23 windows.
